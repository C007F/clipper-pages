Clipper privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Clipper is a private tool run by one person for personal use. It has no other users.

Access: only files and folders Clipper itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope).

Storage: short video clips, references to source videos and run records, inside that Drive folder.

Sharing: Clipper does not collect personal data from anyone else and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties.

Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion: the owner can delete the files in Drive at any time or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
